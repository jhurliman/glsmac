All66 triples and all3168 original button/lock outputs match the [frozen command receipt](behavior/faction-energy-rate-command.md) and its [literal controls](behavior/faction-energy-rate-command-cases.json). There is no invented difficulty cap or rate-change fee.

Buttons immediately update saved faction rates and every owned Base. Enter/Keep closes without another resource pass. Escape/Cancel restores the opening numeric rates through compensating recalculation; it does not restore an entire historical Base snapshot. The window displays actual current engine outputs, including residual Labs at a zero Labs percentage. Its layout is provisional and is not the original complete Social Engineering screen.

Complete talent/drone/riot effects and general citizen/Governor policy remain unfinished; calculating the Psych category does not claim those systems are complete.

During the modal, new conflicting gameplay commands are rejected, previously queued conflicting commands are discarded at dispatch, and physical campaign saves are fenced at the owning save checkpoint.
