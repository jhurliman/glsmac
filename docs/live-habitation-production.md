# Live habitation construction

The ordinary local SMAC production picker and queue now admit Hab Complex
(source24) and Habitation Dome (source25). Default new campaigns register the
installed fac024/fac025 artwork. Costs, maintenance and technology prerequisites
come from the frozen selected FACILITIES rows, rather than the presentation
cost. Dome requires an installed Complex. Completion queue advancement also
sees a privately staged Complex grant; a merely promised queue entry does not
satisfy the provisional port queue editor's current-eligibility admission.

The [original eligibility receipt](behavior/facility-project-build-eligibility.md)
and [completion/population discriminator](behavior/hab-completion-before-population.md)
establish the dependency and grant-before-housing order. Population-rule
preparation reads the registered staged grant without installing it early.
Existing nutrient intake still feeds the population step, and the existing
post-growth resource pass collects the new surplus. Failure before commit
publishes neither the facility nor population/stores/frontend updates.

Focused native validation covers actual picker acceptance, missing technology,
missing Complex, staged-Complex queue admission, local duplicate withholding,
completion of both facilities, cumulative maintenance2/6 and actual saved-game
Start with both grants and the advanced target retained. The actual default GSE
producer/definition handler binds source24/25. Six further native scenarios cover
population7/14, successful or mineral-short completion and late rollback.
Their deliberately controlled center yields surplus4 before growth and2 after;
they discriminate the live staging boundary but do not duplicate the original
receipt's ordinary worked-land fixture. Full snapshot/RNG/frontend queues remain
unchanged on late failure. Existing population, Tanks and facility-restore groups
also pass. Eight affected production/consumer translation-unit analyses pass.

The existing production chooser uses its scrolling list; the provisional queue
choice panel has at most nine simultaneously eligible items and its nine-entry
pending storage bound is unchanged. Governor/Workshop/Help and broader facility
admission remain open inspector work. Existing saves that lack the new facility
presentation definitions retain their saved catalog; this does not invent a
replacement catalog or silently migrate mod definitions. New default campaigns
and saves containing the registered definitions expose the new choices.

This slice does not claim SMAX rules, full ordinary production chooser parity,
or a rendered Hab-completion proof in an unmodified campaign that has not yet
researched its actual prerequisites. Original assets remain external to Git.
