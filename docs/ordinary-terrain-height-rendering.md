# Ordinary terrain heights in the live renderer

Retained standard Planet and its climate publications now supply ordinary land mesh heights from original altitude bands. The finalizer uses the original C/W/N/E/S fan: land centers are 25 pixels per altitude band above band 3; a corner is the floored average of its four land centers, or zero if any contributor is sea or outside the world. Horizontal wrapping and polar clipping follow the admitted Planet topology.

The [original geometry contract](behavior/ordinary-terrain-projection.md), independent reference, and 18 captured semantic contexts are included. The native regression checks all 18 height arrays, including contour-independent geometry, coast, seam, pole, low-relief and raw Planet cases. A second test invokes the actual Finalize module and reads the display and picking mesh vertices, the retained sprite center, and visual RNG state. Native centers stay on the fan position; the old jitter draws are consumed without changing the mesh so later visual randomness keeps its schedule.

The height conversion is relative to the current isometric camera: its screen Y coefficients for world Y/Z have opposite signs and equal magnitude. A full world-Y tile represents 48 native zoom0 pixels. This gives the original height-to-tile ratio without changing gameplay elevation fields or feeding display heights through the gameplay elevation clamp. Base/Unit/terrain-sprite consumers see the same retained land coordinates. Coastal click geometry no longer replaces those coordinates with the old coastline average.

This is ordinary land geometry, not a claim of scene pixel parity. Current camera sizing/zoom selection, named-landmark deformation, discrete native lighting and palette rasterization, terrain UV/art composition and native water depth remain unfinished. Water and maps without retained original altitude-band authority continue through the existing geometry path. The low-relief formula is covered by native cases but its preference is not yet exposed by the live UI; admitted Planet rendering uses the default 25-pixel relief. The saved contour-to-gameplay elevation adapter remains unchanged.

Validation: cached incremental build; two focused native groups (18 original contexts and two real-finalizer probes); targeted C++ correctness analysis of Finalize.cpp. Runtime capture is recorded separately before publication. No new platform checkouts or full platform matrix were required for this geometry iteration.
