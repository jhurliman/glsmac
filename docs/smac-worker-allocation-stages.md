# Automatic worker scoring and assignment stages

`SmacWorkerAllocationRules` provides detached automatic allocation calculations:
effective human governor or AI priorities, a candidate score, strict best/worst
ranking, and a single worker addition or replacement decision. It does not yet
activate canonical worker commands or perform a complete base recalculation.

The implementation follows the original SMAC priority, candidate-score and
worker-step contracts. The test fixture contains 66 literal cases: 22 named
scores, 15 named priority cases, 20 named decisions, seven composed worker-choice
results, one signed AI priority case and one provisional-specialist-budget case.
Each named expected result was checked against the retained original executable
report's `actual` fields. Executable, public fixture and report SHA-256 pins are
in the C++ fixture. No original assets or executable bytes are included.

The seven composition cases exercise this module with resolved center/candidate
yields. Their final selection and intake match complete original intake runs;
the port test does not execute the original availability builder or a complete
port allocator. Candidate-score values generated by the research model are not
used as original observations.

## Preserved behavior

Human governor activation is independent of citizen management. An inactive
human governor yields zero priorities; AI uses the supplied signed personality
values. Mixed human directives retain their original aggression precedence.
Manual replacement uses different weights and must not call this automatic path.

Candidate scoring keeps integer truncation, scarcity adjustments, ordered
zero-yield discounts and separate component divisions. The mineral adjustment
applies to projects after Human Genome Project, not to that first project.
Ranking starts at best zero and worst 9999, and equal scores preserve the earlier
candidate. The caller supplies legal candidates in ascending radius order.

Addition consumes a slot even for clipped coordinates. It requests another scan
when vacancies remain or current food demand is unmet. Replacement requires a
strict score improvement and sufficient food for the next population; it never
removes the center. A rejected replacement restores the visited set, queries
both tiles again and ends the scan. Sea participation remains set after a
rejected sea replacement.

The resource callback is a whole-tile abstraction. It receives staged mask and
intake, and returns owned N/M/E and sea status. It is called once for addition,
old then new for a kept replacement, and old/new/new/old for rollback. It does
not expose the original four resource-selector calls or their intermediate
side effects; its sea marker is the preceding marker. It must not mutate live
state. Exceptions discard the detached result, leaving the input unchanged.

## Admission and remaining integration

Population is bounded to 0..127, slot and specialist counts to 0..128, and
nutrient consumption must be positive. Scoring and decision arithmetic require
representable signed32 intermediates. Overflow is rejected before publication;
these APIs do not claim overflowing original arithmetic. Checked scoring follows
the measured instruction grouping, including combined LEA sums, without adding
artificial overflow checks inside a single original operation.

Negative best indices denote specialist competition and are rejected here.
Existing allocation collection, unavailable-square producers, specialist
competition and final fill, repair, full resource accounting and live publication
remain separate stages. In particular, do not route every valid allocation
through optimization: the original can finish an already complete allocation
before checking food shortages.
